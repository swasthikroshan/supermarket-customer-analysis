# Supermarket Customer & Sales Analysis

## Project Overview
This project analyzes 100 supermarket transactions using Google Sheets to uncover sales trends, customer purchasing behavior, product category performance, and payment preferences.

The project includes KPI calculations, category-wise revenue analysis, customer spending comparisons, payment method analysis, data quality checks, and an interactive-style sales dashboard. The findings are translated into actionable business recommendations to support data-driven decision-making.

## Dataset Description
The dataset contains 100 supermarket transactions used to analyze sales performance and customer purchasing behavior.

Key fields include:
- Transaction ID
- Customer ID
- Product Category
- Total Transaction Amount (INR)
- Payment Method

The dataset supports revenue analysis, customer spending comparisons, product category performance, and payment method evaluation.

- **Dataset size:** 100 transactions
- **Currency:** Indian Rupees (INR)
- **Analysis tool:** Google Sheets

## Objectives
- Calculate key business performance indicators (KPIs).
- Identify the highest-revenue customers.
- Compare revenue across product categories.
- Analyze revenue by payment method.
- Provide actionable business recommendations.

## Key Findings

### Product Category Analysis
- Highest-revenue category: Personal Care — ₹6,370.
- Second-highest category: Household — ₹6,190.
- Lowest-revenue category: Snacks — ₹1,705.

### Customer Analysis
- Highest-revenue customer: C118 — ₹2,790.
- Second-highest revenue customer: C117 — ₹2,635.
- Third-highest revenue customer: C108 — ₹2,215.

### Payment Method Analysis
- UPI revenue: ₹10,565.
- Cash revenue: ₹9,785.
- Card revenue: ₹7,305.
- UPI generated the highest revenue among the three payment methods.

## Business Recommendations
1. Maintain adequate stock of Personal Care and Household products.
2. Review the pricing, promotions, and product range of Snacks.
3. Study top customers' purchasing patterns and encourage repeat purchases.
4. Maintain a smooth UPI payment experience.
5. Test product bundles and cross-selling to increase average transaction value.

## Conclusion
This project demonstrates how spreadsheet analysis, pivot tables, formulas, and charts can transform raw supermarket transaction data into useful business insights.
